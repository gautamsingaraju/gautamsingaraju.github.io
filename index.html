Gautam Singaraju is a Senior Staff Machine Learning Engineer at ServiceNow. He builds production systems for conversational and agentic AI: evaluation and runtime infrastructure, plus Now Assist summarization and reply recommendation. Previously he was Consulting Member of Technical Staff at Oracle, where he owned the machine-learning train-and-serve platform for Oracle Digital Assistant. He is an inventor on 16 granted U.S. patents in conversational machine learning.
